Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

Hi there! I’m Orrin Jones, the developer behind Veridia: The Great Scramble. I wrote this privacy policy to clearly explain what data is collected when you play the game, how it’s used, and how your privacy is protected.

I prefer to keep things simple: I do not know who you are, I don't ask for your personal information, and I do not sell your data.Here is the breakdown of what goes on behind the scenes so you can test and enjoy the game safely!

1. You Play Anonymously. You do not need to create an account, give me an email address, or provide a real name to play. Players are signed into the game completely anonymously.

2. What I Collect (And Why) I want to make Veridia: The Great Scramble the best game it can be. To do this, I use a tool called Firebase Analytics as well as GameAnalytics to track in-app actions—like which buttons you press, which levels you beat, or where you might get stuck.
How it's used: Strictly to fix bugs, balance gameplay, and improve the app.
Data sharing: I do not share this gameplay data with any third parties.
Location: The game itself does not ask for or track your precise GPS location.

3. Ads and Third-Party Services (AdMob)To help support the game's development, I use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Because AdMob serves these ads, they automatically collect some standard device information:What AdMob collects: Your device's advertising ID, IP address, and estimated (coarse) location. They use this to serve you ads that are actually relevant to your region and interests.
AdMob data sharing: AdMob may share this specific advertising data with their own approved third-party ad networks to fill the ad slots in the game.
You can read more about how Google uses this data in their Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children’s Privacy. I do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or anyone else, for that matter!). Because players are completely anonymous and the game has no way for you to share personal information with me, there is no personal data stored or collected.

5. Changes to This PolicyI might update this policy occasionally as I add new features. If I make big changes, I’ll update the "Last Updated" date at the top.

6. Contact me If you have any questions about this policy or the game, feel free to reach out!
